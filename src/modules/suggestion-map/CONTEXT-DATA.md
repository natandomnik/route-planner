# Regional context data

Bundled Natural Earth 1:50m boundaries and 1:10m populated places, public domain:
- https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
- https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-cultural-vectors/
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_boundary_lines_land.geojson
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_10m_populated_places_simple.geojson

Retrieved 2026-10-29. `context-data.js` retains boundary geometry and a disputed flag; populated places retain name, coordinates, population ranking and capital flag. Cities are selected at population >=250,000 or national capitals. Population is historical cartographic ranking, not a current census or evidence of carrier availability. Labels are culled to the viewport and collision-filtered, with larger cities prioritised. Boundaries are generalized, de facto cartography, not legal or street-level guidance; disputed segments use dashed styling. No runtime network request is needed for these layers. Route/suggestion layers stay above them.

## Logistics orientation catalogue

`logistics-data.js` bundles 32 selected European town-level logistics references. Some overlap existing Natural Earth cities and enrich those entries instead of duplicating labels. Town centres are approximate orientation coordinates, not warehouse locations, entrances, postcode centroids or evidence of available trucks. This is a selective catalogue, not an exhaustive European inventory. Nothing is generated by AI or fetched at runtime for these labels.

Sources checked 2026-10-29 (source IDs in each record):
- `glp`: https://eu.glp.com/our-parks/ — named logistics park towns.
- `prologis`: https://www.prologisgermany.de/en/insights/research/europes-most-desireable-logistics-locations and https://www.prologisce.eu/insights/research/themes-shaping-new-location-selection-europe — logistics markets, not live demand.
- `ctp`: https://ctp.eu/find-your-strategic-location/ — Bor and Lipník nad Bečvou.
- `ctp-report`: https://ctp.eu/files/2025/03/CTP-EMTN-2025-Update-Base-Listing-Particulars-2025.pdf — listed industrial/logistics locations.
- `contera`: https://www.conteraparks.cz/en — Teplice, Krupka, Ostrava and Hustopeče.

At zoom 3–6 the layer prioritizes large cities and capitals. Regional logistics centres start at zoom 7–8; smaller towns at zoom 9–10. Existing large-city labels remain geographically useful when zooming in but lose their oversized typography. Logistics references gain collision priority at zoom 8+. Labels are filtered to the visible viewport, collision-checked and capped by viewport area (at most 18/28/40 according to zoom). Route markers stay above the context layer. Existing map tiles can still contain their own place names independently of this overlay.

## Expanded logistics and truck-parking references

The catalogue now contains 44 logistics-town references (including overlaps with Natural Earth) and 47 separate parking records in `parking-data.js`. Added town sources:
- `panattoni-strykow`: https://panattonieurope.com/en-pl/find-properties/panattoni-park-strykow-iv
- `p3-blonie`: https://www.p3parks.com/our-locations/poland/p3-blonie
- `prologis-2026`: https://www.prologis.cn/sites/china/files/documents/2026/02/Prologis%20Europe%20-%20Explore%20Move-in%20Ready%20Space%20-%20Q1%202026%20%28English%29.pdf
- `bremen-invest`: https://www.wfb-bremen.de/en/page/commercial-property/industrial-estates/business-park-bremer-industrie-park
- `rotterdam-port`: https://www.portofrotterdam.com/en/logistics/connections/intermodal-transportation/road-transport/truck-parks

Parking source: https://eu-parkings.eu/sstpas/sstpas-map/, retrieved 2026-10-29. Each record preserves the directory's entry URL and numeric latitude/longitude. Only names, coordinates and reference URLs are bundled; source HTML, contact information, prices and certification claims are not included. Coordinates are directory references, not verified access gates. The imported subset excludes duplicate San Román, conflicting/outlying records for Porta de Barcelona, NORPETROL-JÚNDIZ, Hatvan, Nagykanizsa, Goordijk and Ketenislaan, and an unclear F.A.L. entry. This is a curated snapshot, not a complete or automatically updated directory.

Parking labels use a blue P and start at zoom 10. They share the viewport/collision filters and occupy at most half the label budget, preserving space for towns. Route suggestions remain independent. No runtime API calls, driver tracking or occupancy claims are involved. Parking data does not feed geocoding, route calculation or AI suggestions.
